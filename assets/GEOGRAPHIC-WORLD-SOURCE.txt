Geographic reference basemap provenance

Country outlines: Natural Earth vector data via topojson/world-atlas v2, countries-110m.json.
Source: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/world-atlas@2/countries-110m.json
Source project: https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas

The GeoJSON/TopoJSON was projected to Web Mercator and rendered as a simplified SVG for a deterministic, locally bundled fallback under OpenStreetMap tiles. This drawing is geographical context only, not evidence of an ancestor's location. Location coordinates and GEDCOM family records are maintained separately.
